Research & methods

Interests. Theoretical and computational astrophysics: nonlinear disk hydrodynamics, orbital dynamics, and planet–disk interactions — applied to exoplanets, black holes, accretion processes and distorted accretion disks, the dynamical formation of gravitational-wave sources, and black-hole dynamics in active galactic nuclei. Additional interests in machine learning and large language models.

Numerical methods. N-body integration · grid-based hydrodynamics · smoothed-particle hydrodynamics · molecular dynamics.

Analytical methods. Secular and resonant perturbation theory · linear mode analysis of disks.

Programming. Python · Fortran · C++ · MATLAB.
