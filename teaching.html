Outreach

Working with students outside the usual pipeline is a reminder that educational opportunity, prior experience and level of preparation vary a great deal — and that curiosity and scientific potential are not defined by any of them.

REACH — Research Experiences in Astronomy at CIERA

I took part in REACH at Northwestern University, helping high-school students develop programming skills and gain a first exposure to astronomical research.

Discussion panels in Rwanda

I have twice served, remotely, on discussion panels with students at a girls’ high school in Rwanda, sharing my experience of scientific research and of building an academic career.
